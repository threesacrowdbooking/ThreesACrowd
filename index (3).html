Who We Are

Live Jazz, Locally Rooted

Three's A Crowd Booking connects clients across the Richmond area with vetted local jazz musicians assembled specifically for each event.

Every trio is handpicked — not a house band, not a generic package. We work with you on the lineup, the feel, and the setlist so the music fits the room.

We're based in Richmond and book events throughout Central Virginia. All musicians are local, professional, and passionate about supporting the regional jazz community.

"Thank you for supporting local jazz musicians."

  • Handpicked musicians for each event
  • Fully customizable setlists
  • Richmond-based, locally connected
  • One point of contact, start to finish
  • Available for indoor & outdoor venues
  • Flexible set lengths and configurations

How Booking Works

I

Reach Out

II

We Build Your Trio

We select musicians from our roster that suit your event and put together a proposed lineup.

III

Enjoy the Music
